# Universal Planning Networks (UPN)

- **Authors:** Aravind Srinivas, Allan Jabri, Pieter Abbeel, Sergey Levine, **Chelsea Finn** (UC Berkeley)
- **Group role:** Senior (last) author (Finn); corresponding author is the first author (Srinivas), not a summary-group PI
- **Date published:** 2018-07 (ICML 2018, venue date); arXiv 2018-04-02
- **Venue:** ICML 2018

## Summary
UPN embeds a differentiable gradient-descent planner inside a goal-conditioned policy. An image encoder and a latent forward dynamics model are unrolled over a candidate action sequence, the plan is refined by gradient descent toward the encoded goal image, and the whole planning computation is trained end-to-end with an outer imitation loss. The learned latent space then serves as a goal-distance reward for model-free RL on harder tasks and other robot morphologies.

Scope note: the forward model predicts latent states only (no pixel reconstruction) and is trained for plannability, not prediction accuracy. Included as a latent world model used for planning.

## 3. Datasets
Synthetic expert demonstrations in MuJoCo tasks generated by the authors: point-mass navigation and 3-link reacher with obstacles (fixed or varying obstacles and goals), 7-DoF pushing from poking data, transfer to 4-/5-link reachers and an ant. Not released.

## 4. Evaluation and downstream tasks
- Visual imitation (Fig. 5): UPN beats reactive and autoregressive imitation baselines in low-data regimes; AIL catches up with more data.
- More planning steps at test time (Sec. 4.2): Reacher VOVG success from 38.1% (40 steps) to 64.44% (160 steps) with 20k demos; expert 73.12%.
- RL with UPN-derived rewards (Table 1, reacher with obstacles): fixed goals 69.84% and varying goals 71.12% vs VAE features 20.23% / 24.67%, UPN imitation (160 steps) 45.82% / 47.99%, expert 46.77% / 51.1%.
- Transfer (Fig. 9): UPN rewards outperform VAE, AIL, RIL features for 5-link reacher, point-to-ant, and pushing-from-poking.
- Downstream tasks: imitation, gradient-based planning, reward shaping for RL, cross-morphology transfer.

## 5. Architecture and techniques
- Encoder f_phi (CNN) and latent forward model g_theta (fully connected), x_{t+1} = g(x_t, a_t).
- Inner loop: gradient descent planner on a Huber loss between predicted final latent and goal latent; outer loop: behavior-cloning loss back-propagated through all planning iterations.
- MPC re-planning at test time.

## 6. Conclusions and future work
- Authors: plannable representations learned end-to-end support visual imitation and give transferable, obstacle-aware reward metrics. Future (from text): hierarchical/option-conditioned latent models, other outer objectives such as RL.
- Own observations: all experiments are simulated with synthetic experts; the dynamics model is not evaluated as a predictor of observations.
