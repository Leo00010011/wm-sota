## Summary
V-CDN (Visual Causal Discovery Network) learns a structured, keypoint-based world model from raw video without supervision. It has three parts:
- An unsupervised keypoint detector extracts the state.
- An inference module performs one-shot discovery of a causal summary graph: edge existence, discrete edge type, and continuous edge parameters as hidden confounders.
- A graph recurrent dynamics module predicts future keypoints conditioned on that graph.

The model supports long-horizon prediction, counterfactual "what if" prediction through intervening on the inferred graph, and extrapolation to graphs of unseen size.

## 3. Datasets
Both datasets are synthetic and generated by the authors (App. B):
- **Multi-Body:** Pymunk, 5,000 episodes × 500 frames, 250 held out for test. Five balls; each pair has a one-third probability of no link, a rigid rod or a spring. Spring rest length is sampled from [20, 120].
- **Fabric:** NVIDIA FleX, 2,000 episodes × 300 frames, 200 held out for test. Shirts, pants and towels of varying shape, with forces applied on the contour. Actions are encoded as a 6-D vector.

Extrapolation test sets contain 3, 4, 6 and 7 bodies. No public dataset release was found.

## 4. Evaluation and downstream tasks
- **Causal-graph discovery (Multi-Body, Fig. 4):**
  - Edge-type accuracy over {null, spring, rigid} rises, and entropy falls, as more frames are observed.
  - Inferred continuous parameters correlate strongly with ground truth (spring rest length, rod length).
  - Values are given only as plots.
- **Future prediction (Fig. 6d, Fig. 9):** keypoint MSE is lower than a baseline without the inference module in both Multi-Body and Fabric. In Fabric over 20 steps, the error stays below the baseline (curve only).
- **Extrapolation (Fig. 6):** the model is trained on 5 bodies and generalises without retraining the inference and dynamics modules to 3, 4, 6 and 7 bodies; it is even better on 3 and 4. A new perception module is needed for each body count.
- **Counterfactual prediction (Fig. 7):** interventions on rest length, rod length and edge type, evaluated against the simulator at T+30. The model extrapolates to parameter ranges outside training.
- **Downstream tasks:** unsupervised structure discovery, long-horizon prediction and counterfactual prediction. No control or planning.

## 5. Architecture and techniques
- **Perception:** a Transporter-style unsupervised keypoint detector (Kulkarni et al.). It uses a 5-block CNN feature extractor and detector with spatial softmax, a refiner, and a pixel L2 reconstruction loss. Adam with learning rate 1e-3 for about 240k iterations.
- **Inference module:**
  - Interaction-Network-style GNN per frame over a fully connected graph.
  - 1-D temporal CNNs with max-pooling.
  - A GNN that predicts the edge-type distribution, using Gumbel-Softmax; type 1 is the "null" edge.
  - A GNN that predicts continuous edge parameters for the non-null edges.
- **Dynamics:** a graph recurrent network that outputs a Gaussian (mean and covariance) for each keypoint's next position. In Fabric, the action is concatenated to every keypoint.
- **Objective:** an ELBO-style loss, with prediction log-likelihood minus the KL divergence of the edge posterior from a factorised prior (Eq. 9).
- **Training:** Adam with learning rate 1e-4 (Multi-Body) or 5e-4 (Fabric) for about 300k iterations on a single TITAN Xp. Parameter count not stated.

## 6. Conclusions and future work
- **Authors:** V-CDN discovers dependency structures and causal mechanisms end-to-end from images without supervision. They hope it can "facilitate future studies of more generalizable visual reasoning systems". The broader-impact section names untouched aspects: sophisticated dependencies, and practical issues arising from sampling rates.
- **My own observations:**
  - Only 2D simulated scenes are used, and the cloth graph has no ground truth.
  - The perception module must be retrained for each number of bodies.
  - No action-conditioned planning is shown, so the model is used for prediction only.
  - Results are mostly plots with no tables.
